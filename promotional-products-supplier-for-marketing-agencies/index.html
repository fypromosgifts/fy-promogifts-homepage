Procurement Checklist

Compare Facts Before Comparing Quotes

A useful promotional products quotation should identify what is included and what still needs confirmation. This prevents two apparently similar quotes from hiding different materials, branding routes or packing assumptions.

DecisionWhat the agency should provideWhat FY PromoGifts confirmsWhy it changes the quote
Product specificationIntended use, audience and preferred styleMaterial, dimensions, color route and available configurationSimilar-looking products can use different materials and construction.
Logo decorationVector artwork, brand colors and preferred finishSuitable method, printable area and artwork limitationsScreen printing, engraving, UV printing and embroidery have different setup needs.
Order quantityEstimated units or number of completed kitsProduct-led MOQ and whether packaging has a separate MOQThe highest component MOQ can determine the practical kit quantity.
Sample routeApproval requirement and decision deadlineAvailable blank, decorated or pre-production sample routeSampling method affects approval timing and what can be checked.
Packaging and kittingBulk packing, individual packing or presentation requirementBox fit, insert, card, labeling and assembly scopePackaging dimensions affect assembly work and shipping volume.
Delivery planDestination, need-by date and delivery-point countProduction and transport assumptions shown separatelyA realistic schedule depends on the approved specification and shipping route.

Evidence boundary: prices, certifications, stock, exact production time and shipping time are not assumed on this page. They are confirmed against the selected product, artwork, quantity, packaging and destination before order approval.

Buyer Questions

Agency Sourcing FAQ

Answers are kept product-led because MOQ, production and shipping depend on the final specification.

Can I send a client brief without choosing products first?

Yes. Share the audience, objective, quantity, destination, deadline, budget direction and brand assets. We can recommend a practical product and packaging direction.

Can several items be combined into one branded kit?

Yes. Drinkware, notebooks, bags, pens, event items, cards and packaging can be combined into a coordinated kit.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Low MOQ options are available for selected products. Actual MOQ depends on the item, logo method, color requirements and packaging.

How quickly can I receive a mockup?

A free digital mockup can be prepared within 2 hours after receiving the logo and clear project requirements.

Which procurement details are confirmed before quotation?

We review the product specification, decoration method, quantity, packaging, sample route, destination and required date. Unknown details are listed for confirmation instead of being filled with assumptions.

Can agencies request samples or pre-production approval?

Sample and approval options depend on the selected product, decoration method and schedule. We confirm the available route and any related cost before the order is placed.
