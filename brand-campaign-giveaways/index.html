Brand Consistency System

Repeat the Brand Without Making Every Item Identical

A campaign kit looks stronger when color, logo scale, materials and packaging work together. We can recommend the right branding method for each surface while keeping one overall visual direction.

Available methods may include embroidery, screen printing, UV printing, laser engraving, debossing, printed campaign cards, sleeves and packaging labels.

  • Hero LogoPrimary placement on the backpack, tote or main drinkware item.
  • Supporting MarksSmaller repeated logo placement on notebooks, pens and cards.
  • Campaign ColorCoordinate product colors and accents within realistic material tolerances.

FAQ

Brand Campaign Giveaway Questions

Practical answers for agency, event and promotional merchandise buyers before requesting a quotation.

Are these fixed campaign kits?

No. The three levels are starting points. Products, colors, logo methods, campaign cards and packaging can all be adjusted before quotation.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Many coordinated campaign projects fall in the 200?500 set range, but actual MOQ is product-led. Each item, logo method, color requirement and packaging component must be checked.

Can all products use exactly the same color?

We can coordinate the palette using available product colors and materials. Exact color matching varies across fabric, metal, plastic, paper and printing processes, so tolerances are confirmed before production.

Can an agency source on behalf of a client?

Yes. Share the client brief, campaign palette, audience, quantity, destination and deadline. We can recommend a practical product and packaging direction for review.

Can I choose products outside the examples?

Yes. Use the examples as a starting point, browse the catalog or send a product direction. The final mix should fit the campaign, budget and distribution method.

Can you customize campaign cards and packaging?

Yes. Options can include printed campaign cards, labels, sleeves, mailers, kraft boxes, magnetic boxes and individual kit assembly, depending on quantity and schedule.
