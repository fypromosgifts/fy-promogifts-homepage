Buyer Questions

Trade Show Giveaway FAQ

Need more ideas? Read our trade show buyer guide or send the event goal and quantity for a custom recommendation.

What products are commonly used at US and European trade shows?

Common products include branded lanyards, badge holders, tote bags, reusable drinkware, event apparel, phone stands, charging cables, power banks, wristbands, stickers, patches and staff bags.

Can the displayed trade show kits be changed?

Yes. The displayed kits are starting concepts. Products, colors, logo placement, quantities and packing methods can be adjusted before quotation.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ is product-led and varies by item, color, branding method and packaging. Low MOQ is available for selected products, while mixed-product kits are reviewed item by item.

Can you provide more sustainable trade show giveaways?

Yes. Reusable products, recycled-material bags, durable drinkware, reduced packaging and practical items can be prioritized for programs with sustainability requirements.

How early should trade show giveaways be ordered?

Share the event city, quantity and must-arrive date as early as possible. Product selection, artwork approval, production and shipping time must all be confirmed before the event.

Can I request a digital mockup or physical sample?

A digital mockup can be prepared after receiving the logo, selected products and project requirements. Physical sample availability and cost depend on the products and customization methods.
