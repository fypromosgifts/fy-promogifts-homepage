Buyer Questions

Trade Show Giveaway FAQ

Final recommendations depend on audience, quantity, destination and deadline.

What products work in a trade show giveaway kit?

Useful options include tote bags, drinkware, notebooks, pens, lanyards, badges, tech accessories and branded packaging.

Can different products be packed together?

Yes. Products, printed cards and packaging can be coordinated into attendee packs, speaker kits, VIP gifts or staff kits.

How should I plan around the event date?

Share the destination and event date early. Production time and shipping time should be confirmed separately against the selected products and logo methods.
