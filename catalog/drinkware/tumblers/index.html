Choose a tumbler around the way it will be used

A tumbler is most useful when its capacity, material and lid format match the recipient's routine. The 40 oz insulated handle tumbler is designed for outdoor events, wellness campaigns and employee gifting where a large capacity and easy carry matter. The 380 ml or 510 ml stainless steel travel coffee tumbler is a more compact option for commuting and daily office use. The bamboo-lid glass tumbler offers a clear, modern presentation with a reusable straw for office, cafe and event programs.

Tell us whether the product will be shipped to individual recipients, handed out at an event or packed inside a larger gift kit. That choice affects product dimensions, carton planning and the packing route. Also share the destination and must-arrive date so production and transit can be reviewed as one schedule.

Custom tumbler sourcing FAQ

Which tumbler is best for an employee or event program?

Choose by use case, capacity, material, distribution method and packing. A handled 40 oz tumbler suits outdoor and wellness programs, a smaller travel tumbler suits daily commuting, and a glass tumbler offers a different presentation for office or cafe campaigns.

Can FY PromoGifts apply a logo to tumblers?

Yes. Available routes in this catalog include logo printing and, on compatible stainless steel products, laser engraving. The final method and print area are confirmed against the selected product and artwork.

What is the MOQ for custom tumblers?

MOQ is confirmed for the selected product, decoration method, color and packaging. Send the target quantity and deadline for a product-specific quotation rather than relying on one category-wide MOQ.
