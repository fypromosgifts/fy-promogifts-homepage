Choose the notebook around the program

A branded notebook works best when the format matches the moment in which it is received. An A5 PU notebook and pen combination is practical for company meetings and business gifts. An integrated pen holder helps keep an onboarding or conference pack organized. An elastic-band closure gives a familiar everyday format for welcome kits, internal campaigns and employee programs.

The current catalog also includes a metal rollerball pen and a magnetic bookmark, so the stationery can be quoted as a standalone item or assembled into a coordinated office set. Share whether recipients will receive the product at one location, through individual shipment or inside a larger gift box because the distribution route affects packing and carton planning.

Cover material, branding and artwork

The listed notebook range uses PU covers in A5 formats. Depending on the selected product, options include logo printing, debossing and custom color. Debossing creates a restrained cover impression, while printed artwork may suit a more visible campaign mark. The right route depends on the cover surface, artwork detail, color requirements and quantity.

Provide vector artwork in AI, EPS, PDF or SVG format where possible. A digital mockup should confirm logo position, scale and color before production. When exact texture, imprint depth or color matching is important, ask about a physical sample route. Product and artwork review must happen before any decoration method or result is treated as final.

MOQ, timing and an accurate notebook quote

MOQ and production timing are product-led rather than category-wide. Stock color, cover construction, decoration, accessories, packaging and order quantity all affect the workable route. An accurate request should include quantity, preferred notebook style, logo file, color direction, packaging, destination and need-by date. If a pen must match the notebook or sit inside a fitted box, include that from the beginning.

For deadline-sensitive programs, work backward from the must-arrive date. Allow time for artwork review, mockup approval, any sample, bulk production, packing and shipping. A simple standard-color notebook with one logo can follow a different path from a color-matched notebook and fully printed presentation box.

Build notebooks into onboarding and client kits

Notebooks pair naturally with pens, drinkware, tech accessories, cards and reusable bags. For a fuller project direction, review the employee onboarding kit guide, compare corporate gift kits, or plan the mix in the Kit Studio. If presentation is central to the brief, use the custom packaging guide to define the box, insert and printed components.

Custom notebook sourcing FAQ

What notebook options are available for company branding?

The current range includes A5 PU notebooks with a pen, an integrated pen holder or an elastic-band closure. Available branding routes include logo printing, debossing and custom color, subject to the selected product and quantity.

Can custom notebooks be included in an onboarding kit?

Yes. A notebook can be paired with a pen, drinkware, welcome card, tech accessory or presentation box. Share the complete kit direction so dimensions, protection and pack-out can be checked together.

What information is needed for a notebook quote?

Provide quantity, preferred size and cover style, logo artwork, branding method, color direction, packaging, destination and need-by date. MOQ and timing are then confirmed for that exact configuration.
