Custom umbrella FAQ

Answers before you request a quote.

These are the procurement points that make the umbrella format, branding, pack-out and delivery direction specific enough to review.

What are custom promotional umbrellas?

Custom promotional umbrellas are branded compact, automatic, golf or full-size umbrellas used for outdoor events, employee programs, client gifts and everyday visibility. The workable direction is shaped by canopy format, artwork, print route, quantity, sleeve or packaging needs, destination and timing.

Which umbrella format suits a trade show or outdoor event?

Choose by how the umbrella will be carried and used. Compact folding umbrellas suit commuter and welcome-kit programs, while automatic and golf umbrellas give a larger canopy area for outdoor events, hospitality or client gifting. The final choice should be checked against the branding area and delivery brief.

How does logo artwork work across umbrella panels?

Artwork is reviewed against the open canopy, individual panel seams, viewing direction and print area before production. A logo can be planned for one or more panels, but the practical layout depends on the selected umbrella construction, fabric and decoration route.

Which print method is right for an umbrella canopy?

Screen printing, digital transfer and heat-transfer routes can be considered for compatible umbrella fabrics and artwork. The right route depends on the canopy material, artwork detail, colour treatment, placement, quantity and production requirements.

What should I include in an umbrella inquiry?

Include the use case, preferred umbrella format, estimated quantity, canopy colour, logo file or artwork, number of branded panels, sleeve or packaging needs, delivery destination and need-by date. A reference image is useful when you have one.
