FAQ

What logo method works best on canvas?

Screen printing is common for bold logos, while embroidery works well for a more premium texture.

Can tote bags be made in custom sizes?

Yes, custom sizes are possible, though MOQ and lead time may increase.

Can the tote bag be part of a larger kit?

Yes. It is one of the easiest products to pair with drinkware, stationery, lanyards, and printed inserts.
