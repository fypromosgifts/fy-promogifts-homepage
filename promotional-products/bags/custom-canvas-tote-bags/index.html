Choosing a Canvas Tote for Your Brief

Canvas tote specifications work best when they start with the event use and pack-out, rather than a generic product name. Use these four decisions to make the request easier to review.

What needs to fit inside?

Start with brochures, samples, drinkware or the full kit so the finished bag dimensions can be reviewed against the actual contents.

Choose the branding panel

Share the logo, artwork detail and preferred placement so the decoration route can be matched to the selected fabric panel.

Confirm the construction

Handle length, gusset, closure, inside label and insert requirements affect the finished bag specification and should be listed before approval.

Plan the pack-out

Confirm whether bags ship flat, hold other products or need individual packing before requesting a quotation.

FAQ

What logo method works best on canvas?

Screen printing is common for bold logos, while embroidery works well for a more premium texture. The practical option depends on the selected bag panel and artwork.

Can tote bags be made in custom sizes?

Yes, custom sizes are possible, though MOQ and timing may change with the final fabric and construction.

Can the tote bag be part of a larger kit?

Yes. It can be planned to carry drinkware, stationery, lanyards, printed inserts or the full kit.

What should I include in a canvas tote bag brief?

Share the intended contents, preferred size or bag format, colour, logo file, quantity, destination and must-arrive date. This lets the bag construction and branding route be reviewed together.
