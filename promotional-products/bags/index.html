FAQ

Can bags be used as packaging for kits?

Yes. Tote bags and drawstring bags are often used as reusable packaging for event and employee gift kits.

Can you match brand colors?

Many bag styles support fabric color selection or printed color matching, depending on MOQ and material.

Do you offer eco-friendly bag options?

Yes. Canvas, cotton, recycled, and reusable styles can be selected for sustainability-focused campaigns.
