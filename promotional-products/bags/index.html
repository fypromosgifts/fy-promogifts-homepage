Custom bags FAQ

Questions to settle before the quote.

These answers help turn an initial bag idea into a production-ready brief.

What is the minimum order for custom bags?

Most bag projects start from 100 pieces. The final minimum depends on the selected format, fabric, decoration route and packaging requirements.

Which logo method works best for a custom bag?

Screen printing, heat transfer, embroidery and woven patches are reviewed against the bag fabric, logo detail, placement and order quantity before the finish is confirmed.

Can custom bags be part of a gift kit?

Yes. A bag can be coordinated with selected drinkware, stationery, apparel or presentation packaging. The final product mix, pack-out and delivery plan are confirmed against the project brief.

What should I send for a custom bag quote?

Send the bag style, target quantity, logo or artwork, preferred fabric or colour, destination and required date. These details let us check a workable production route.
