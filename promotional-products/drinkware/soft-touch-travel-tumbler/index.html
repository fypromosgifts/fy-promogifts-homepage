Quick answer for buyers

Why use a compact travel tumbler as branded merchandise?

This format fits common workday routines—coffee on the way in, water at a desk and an easy reusable gift at an event. A softer colour story can make the item feel like a useful product first, while the logo remains part of the object rather than the whole message.

Which logo method is best for a soft-touch tumbler?
