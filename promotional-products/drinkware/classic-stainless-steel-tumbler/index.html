Quick answer for buyers

Why start a branded drinkware brief with a classic tumbler?

The uncomplicated form is flexible: it can work as an office giveaway, conference product or a more premium part of a gift kit. Its broad, clean body also gives a logo enough room to read without relying on a trend-specific feature.

Build a useful brief

Use the format to make the logo feel intentional.

Surface & contrast

Pick a body colour that helps the artwork read clearly. A simple mark, detailed colour art and an engraved effect require different confirmation steps.

Capacity & lid

Specify the drink use and capacity direction first; those decisions influence which product and lid combination is practical.

Protection & distribution

For bulk event distribution, individual mailers or gift kits, plan the packing route with the tumbler instead of adding it at the end.

Can I add a colour logo to a stainless steel tumbler?

Yes, where the selected coating and artwork support a compatible printing route. We check the exact product before confirming method and placement.

Is laser engraving permanent?

It can be a durable choice for compatible coated or stainless surfaces, but the finish and resulting contrast must be checked on the actual item.

Can these tumblers be used in gift kits?

Yes. Share the companion items, box dimensions, card or insert needs and total quantity so the appropriate packing route can be reviewed.
