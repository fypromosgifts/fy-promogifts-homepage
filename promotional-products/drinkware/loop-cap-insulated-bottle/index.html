What is a custom loop cap insulated bottle best for?

It is a practical custom water-bottle choice when the recipient will carry it between a desk, commute, gym or event. The loop cap adds a simple grab point, while a calm matte body leaves room for a brand mark without turning the product into a one-time giveaway.
