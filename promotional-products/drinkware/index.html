Custom drinkware FAQ

Answers before you request a quote.

These are the practical procurement points that determine the product route, decoration method, production timing and final quotation.

What is the MOQ for custom promotional drinkware?

The six selected drinkware models on this page start from 100 pieces. Minimum quantities for other models depend on the product, stock, decoration method, colour and packaging.

How quickly can custom drinkware be produced?

The selected stock-ready range can be produced within one week after artwork approval and stock confirmation. International shipping time is quoted separately by destination.

Which logo methods are available for branded drinkware?

Available routes include cylindrical UV printing, laser engraving, screen printing, high-temperature decal and adhesive labels. The compatible method depends on the drinkware material, coating, shape, artwork and quantity.

What details are needed for a custom drinkware quote?

Send the product or capacity, target quantity, logo or artwork, delivery destination, need-by date and any packaging requirement.

How should a buyer choose between a mug, bottle and tumbler?

Start with the recipient use, preferred capacity, lid or handle requirement, branding area, packing plan and delivery route. These details narrow the practical product and decoration options.
