Selection checklist

Five decisions that change the bottle quote.

Share these details early so a supplier can recommend a workable product and branding route instead of quoting an incomplete configuration.

Recipient use

Desk work, commuting, outdoor activity, a conference, wellness program or gift kit each suggest a different capacity, lid and carry preference.

Bottle format

Confirm capacity, material direction, color, lid or straw needs, and any handling or carry features that matter to the recipient.

Logo treatment

Provide the source artwork and required colors. Final print area and method depend on the actual bottle surface and shape.

Packaging

Clarify bulk distribution, presentation set or individual shipment. Each route changes protection and packing needs.

Delivery plan

Quantity, destination and must-arrive date determine which product and production route is practical. Transit is reviewed separately from production.

Complete brief

  • Capacity or bottle example
  • Quantity and color breakdown
  • Logo file and placement
  • Destination and date

Custom water bottle FAQ

Can I order different bottle capacities in one project?

Capacity mix depends on selected products, color requirements, decoration method and quantity for each configuration. Share the required breakdown so the practical route can be confirmed.

What logo method is best for custom water bottles?

The best method depends on material, coating, shape, artwork and desired finish. Confirm the product surface and available print area before approving the route.

Do custom water bottles need a physical sample?

A digital mockup is useful for artwork direction. A physical sample may be useful when material, color, logo finish, lid function or packaging fit needs to be checked before bulk production.

What do I need to send for a quote?

Send desired capacity or product direction, target quantity, logo file, preferred color, delivery destination, need-by date and any packaging requirements.
