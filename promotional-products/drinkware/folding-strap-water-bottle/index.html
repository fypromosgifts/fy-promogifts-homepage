Quick answer for buyers

Why put a folding strap on a branded water bottle?

A carry feature can make a reusable bottle more practical for a commuter or event attendee without making it look overloaded. This direction works when the brand needs a product for movement, travel or outdoor use that still presents a clean surface for a logo.
