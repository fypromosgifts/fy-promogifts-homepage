Quick answer for buyers

When does a top-handle tumbler add value to a branded program?

It makes sense when the product will travel between work, activities and home. The upper carry handle creates a practical signal of mobility, while the larger tumbler body can make the custom logo visible in a way compact desk tumblers cannot.
