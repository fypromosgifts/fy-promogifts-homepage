Quick answer for buyers

How should you choose the capacity for a branded water bottle?

Choose the size based on how people will actually use it. A compact format suits light travel or welcome kits; a mid-size bottle fits daily desk use; and the larger direction suits longer outdoor or active routines. Confirm the capacity before planning the logo and packaging.
