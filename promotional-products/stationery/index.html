Custom stationery FAQ

Answers before you request a quote.

These are the procurement points that make the product, branding, packaging and delivery direction specific enough to review.

What is custom promotional stationery?

Custom promotional stationery brings notebooks, pens and presentation sets into a branded program. Start with the recipient and use case, then define the format, material, logo placement, quantity, packaging, destination and need-by date. Those details determine the practical production route.

How should I choose between notebooks, pens and presentation sets?

Choose by how the recipient will use the item and how it will be presented. A notebook may suit training or meetings, a pen can support an everyday office program, and a coordinated set can create a more considered recognition, onboarding or client-gift direction.

Which logo methods can be considered for stationery?

Possible routes can include printed covers, foil or debossed cover details, pen marking and printed packaging. The compatible option depends on the selected material, construction, artwork, color requirements and quantity.

How are MOQ and timing confirmed for a stationery project?

MOQ and timing are product-led. They are confirmed after the selected format, material, artwork, packaging, quantity and delivery destination are reviewed. Production and transit should be treated as separate parts of the schedule.

What should I include in a stationery inquiry?

Include the use case, recipient group, preferred product direction, estimated quantity, logo file, material or color preferences, packaging requirements, delivery destination and need-by date.
