Choose the packaging route before the product list

Ready-to-brand presentation

Use a stock box, bag or mailer when speed and a clean finish matter. Add a logo sticker, sleeve or insert card after the product dimensions are chosen.

Gift-box presentation

Match the box structure, insert and message card to the product mix so each component arrives protected and presented together.

Shipping-ready mailers

Plan the outer mailer and internal protection around the delivery route, especially when kits are sent to more than one destination.

Fully custom packaging

Confirm product dimensions, artwork, packing sequence and approval steps before choosing a custom box route.

FAQ

Can packaging be customized with our logo?

Yes. Common options include printed boxes, stickers, paper bags, sleeves, and insert cards.

Can packaging be added to existing products?

Yes. Packaging can be planned around the selected product size, quantity, and presentation goal.

Is custom packaging required?

No. For budget-sensitive projects, standard packaging plus a branded card or sticker can still look polished.
