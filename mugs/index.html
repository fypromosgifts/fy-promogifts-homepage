Questions buyers ask

Answers before you request a quote.

Include any special recipient, packaging or delivery need in your inquiry so we can check the most relevant route.

What is the MOQ for custom ceramic mug gift sets?

The confirmed starting points are 40 sets for MU023, 60 sets for DR004 and 100 sets for MU024. A different logo method, packaging route or configuration can have a different MOQ.

Can the mug set carry our logo?

Yes. MU023 uses screen printing on the mug. DR004 and MU024 use a high-temperature fired decal route. Final artwork placement is confirmed after review.

Can the gift box be customized?

The regular presentation uses a standard fixed gift box. Custom box artwork is available from 500 pieces for the listed mug gift-set routes.

What should I send to request a quote?

Send your selected set, estimated quantity, logo or artwork, required delivery date and destination. We can then confirm the applicable configuration and quote.
