Common promotional gift buyer questions

Fast answers about MOQ, samples, mockups, mixed-product sourcing, production timing and shipping before you request a quote.

What is the MOQ for custom promotional gifts?

The MOQ depends on the product type, logo process, packaging method, and whether the item is sourced as a single product or as part of a custom gift kit. For many common promotional items such as mugs, pens, tote bags, notebooks, keychains, towels, and simple event giveaways, we can usually support flexible low MOQ options. For fully customized gift boxes, special colors, molded items, or complex packaging, the MOQ may be higher because factories need to set up printing, materials, and packaging production.

If you are not sure whether your project can start from 100 pcs, 300 pcs, or 500 pcs, you can send us your target quantity, logo file, budget, and deadline. We will help check realistic options instead of only giving a fixed MOQ. For mixed-product gift kits, we can also suggest items that work well together within your order quantity. This is especially helpful for agencies that need one campaign kit but must balance brand impact, storage space, and shipping cost.

Can I get a sample or mockup before placing a bulk order?

Yes. For most custom promotional gifts, we can provide a free digital mockup within 2 hours after receiving your logo, product idea, quantity, and basic requirements. A digital mockup helps you check logo position, approximate print size, product color, and overall gift kit presentation before moving forward.

For physical samples, availability depends on the product. Some standard items can be sampled quickly, while fully customized colors, packaging, or special printing may require a sample fee and longer preparation time. In many B2B projects, buyers first confirm the digital mockup, estimated cost, and production timeline, then decide whether a physical sample is necessary. This helps save time when the event date or campaign deadline is close. If several items are sourced together, we can also show how the products, box, insert card, and logo treatment look as one coordinated kit before sample approval. This gives purchasing teams, agencies, and event planners a clearer basis for internal approval.

How long does production and shipping take for custom promotional gifts?

Production time usually depends on the product category, order quantity, logo process, packaging complexity, and factory workload. For many standard promotional products with logo printing, production can often be completed in about 5-10 days after artwork approval. More complex items, custom gift boxes, special materials, or large-volume orders may require a longer lead time.

Shipping time depends on the destination country and shipping method. Air shipping is faster and works better for urgent events, while sea shipping is more cost-effective for larger orders. If your project has a fixed event date, trade show date, employee onboarding schedule, or holiday campaign deadline, please share it at the beginning. We will recommend realistic product and shipping options based on your timeline, instead of only quoting the cheapest method. For mixed-product sourcing, we also check whether every item can finish together before confirming the final plan, because one delayed component can affect the whole gift kit.

Can I order different products in one custom gift kit?

Yes. A custom gift kit can include different products such as tumblers, notebooks, pens, tote bags, tech accessories, welcome cards and custom packaging.

The minimum quantity is normally determined by the MOQ of each customized product rather than the total number of items inside the kit. For example, a 100-set employee welcome kit requires 100 pieces of every included product. If one selected item has a 500-piece MOQ, we may recommend a similar stocked product, a different logo method or branding the packaging instead of the individual item.

We can also combine custom-printed products with unprinted stock items to keep the project flexible.

Send us the required kit quantity, target budget, recipient type, preferred products and delivery country. We can recommend a practical product combination and prepare a digital kit mockup before production.

What information is required for an accurate quotation?

For an accurate quotation, please provide as much of the following information as possible:

  • Product name, photo or product link
  • Required quantity
  • Preferred product color
  • Logo file
  • Number of logo colors
  • Printing position
  • Required packaging
  • Destination country and postal code
  • Required delivery date
  • Whether a physical sample is needed

A vector logo file such as AI, EPS, SVG or editable PDF is preferred. A high-resolution PNG may also be suitable for an initial digital mockup.

If you have not selected a product yet, you can provide your intended use, target budget, recipient type and estimated quantity. We can then recommend suitable promotional products instead of quoting items that do not match your project.

Shipping should be calculated after the product, quantity and packaging are confirmed because carton volume and delivery speed can significantly affect the final cost.

Can you produce a digital logo mockup before ordering?

Yes. We can prepare a free digital mockup within 2 hours after receiving the selected product, logo file and basic customization requirements.

The mockup can show:

  • Approximate logo size
  • Logo position
  • Product color
  • Printing direction
  • Basic packaging artwork
  • The arrangement of products inside a gift kit

A digital mockup helps buyers check the overall visual direction before confirming the order.

However, a digital mockup is not the same as a physical sample. Screen colors, product materials, surface texture and actual printing results may look slightly different in production.

A physical sample may be recommended for large orders, detailed multicolor logos, exact brand-color requirements, complex printing methods or fully customized packaging.

Please send your logo, selected product, preferred color, quantity and printing position to begin the mockup.

What affects the MOQ of custom promotional products?

The MOQ of a custom promotional product can be affected by several factors:

  • Product stock availability
  • Product material
  • Product color
  • Logo printing method
  • Number of logo colors
  • Number of different designs
  • Quantity required per color
  • Individual name personalization
  • Custom packaging
  • Fully custom product shapes or specifications

Many standard promotional products may start at around 100 pieces, while some stocked products can support smaller quantities. Fully custom colors, molded products, special packaging or multiple design variations may require a higher MOQ.

The product MOQ and printing MOQ may also be different. A product may be available in stock, but the printing process may require a minimum production quantity.

To identify the most suitable option, send us the required quantity, product type, logo and packaging requirements. We can compare available products and suggest alternatives that better match a lower-quantity project.

Can different colors or designs be mixed in one order?

In many cases, standard product colors can be mixed within one order, but a minimum quantity per color may apply.

For example, an order of 500 tumblers may be divided into several available colors more easily than an order of 100 tumblers divided into ten colors.

Different logo designs may also be possible, but additional printing setup charges or minimum quantities may apply to each design. The supplier needs to know:

  • Total order quantity
  • Quantity per product color
  • Number of logo designs
  • Number of printing colors
  • Whether the logo position changes
  • Whether individual names are required
  • Whether the packaging is the same for every group

Using the same logo and printing position across several standard product colors is normally more cost-effective than changing the artwork for every group.

Send us the quantity breakdown before ordering so we can confirm the available colors, printing requirements and final cost.

Are tote bags or drinkware better for trade show giveaways?

Tote bags are better when visitors need to carry brochures, samples and other booth materials. Drinkware is better when the goal is a higher perceived value and longer daily use after the event.

What low-cost giveaways work for large events?

Low-cost giveaways work best when they are still useful. Pens, lanyards, stickers, keychains, simple tote bags, badges and small notebooks can support large event distribution without losing all practical value.

Which promotional products have the best long-term use?

Products with daily utility usually have stronger long-term use. Drinkware, bags, notebooks, pens, apparel, lanyards and desk items are easier for recipients to keep because they fit normal work, travel or event routines.
