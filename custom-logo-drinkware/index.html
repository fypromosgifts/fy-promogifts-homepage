Custom logo drinkware FAQ

Can you make a mockup before production?

Yes. Logo mockups can usually be prepared after receiving artwork, item preference and quantity.

Can drinkware be packed as a gift set?

Yes. Packaging options can include individual boxes, branded sleeves, kraft boxes, cards or mixed-product kits.

What logo file should I send?

Vector files such as AI, PDF, EPS or SVG are best for production. PNG or JPG can be used for initial mockups.
