Buyer Questions

Client Gift Set FAQ

Need more ideas? Read our client appreciation gift guide or send us the audience and budget for a custom recommendation.

What products work well for client appreciation gifts?

Useful products such as backpacks, tote bags, insulated cups, ceramic mugs, umbrellas and foldable organizers work well because recipients can continue using them after the gifting moment.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ is product-led and varies by item, logo method, color and packaging. Low MOQ is available for selected products, while mixed-product sets are reviewed item by item.

Can the products and colors be changed?

Yes. The displayed sets are starting concepts. Products, colors, logo placement, message cards and packaging can be adjusted before quotation.

Is gift box packaging required?

No. Packaging is optional. Products can be packed in a tote, a practical mailer, an individual box or bulk cartons depending on presentation, shipping and budget requirements.

Can I request a sample or digital mockup?

A digital mockup can be prepared after receiving the logo, selected products and project requirements. Physical sample availability and cost depend on the individual products and customization methods.
