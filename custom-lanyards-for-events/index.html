Lanyard planning tips

  • Use clear badge holders when attendee names must be visible.
  • Use sublimation for full-color artwork or repeated patterns.
  • Use simple logo print for standard event staff and visitor lanyards.
  • Match clip or hook style to the badge or card holder.
