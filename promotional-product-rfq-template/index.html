Promotional Product RFQ Template: Send Suppliers a Complete Brief

Use one clear request-for-quote brief when you need suppliers to price the same promotional product scope. It keeps product, artwork, packing and arrival details together so the replies are easier to compare.

Before you ask for a quote

Use the same decision fields for every supplier.

An RFQ is most useful when it describes the project you actually need, not just a product name. The details below help suppliers flag what is confirmed and what still needs a decision.

01 / Product

Reference the item

Add a product link, photo or clear description, then state the quantity, colour, size and variants.

02 / Artwork

Describe the logo

Attach the available file and note the print area, approximate colour count and preferred placement.

03 / Pack-out

State what ships together

List individual packaging, insert cards, mixed-product kit assembly or transit-packing needs.

04 / Delivery

Work backwards from arrival

Include the destination, postal code when known, must-arrive date and whether a sample or proof is required.

A useful alternative

Product not selected yet?

Do not force a product guess into the brief. Instead, state the project outcome and the choices that matter.

  • Audience and intended use
  • Quantity and budget direction
  • Event date and delivery destination
  • Preferred style, material or brand colours
  • Whether it needs to work as part of a kit

Compare like with like

Check the response, not only the headline price.

Suppliers can quote different constructions, logo routes or packing assumptions for the same product name. Use this small check before deciding which reply is actually comparable.

Product specificationProduct reference, material, colour, dimensions and included components match the requested brief.

Buyer questions

RFQ template FAQ

These answers support the template above. Product availability, logo routes, minimums and timing are confirmed against the final specification.

What should I include in a promotional product RFQ?

Include the product or reference link, quantity and variants, artwork and placement, packaging, destination, must-arrive date and any sample requirement. If the product is not selected, include the use case, recipient, budget direction and quantity instead.

Should I include a target budget in an RFQ?

A budget direction is optional but useful when the product has not been selected. It helps a supplier recommend options in the right range without treating it as a fixed final price.

Can I request a quote before I have the final logo file?

Yes. Share the planned logo position, approximate colour count and available artwork. The final logo file and decoration route should be confirmed before production approval.
