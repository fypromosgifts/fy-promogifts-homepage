# Google research: non-IP promotional gifts Top 10 — 2026-09-01

## Scope and method

Target market: English-language international B2B buyers, especially agencies, events, employee programs and corporate gifting. “Google Top 10” is interpreted as products supported by current sources discoverable through Google, not as a claim that Google publishes product sales rankings.

Ranking inputs:

1. ASI 2026 International Ad Impressions Study: products international respondents would be most excited to receive.
2. ASI/PPAI 2024–2025 distributor sales mix: what buyers actually purchase.
3. PPAI 2025–2026 retention and daily-use research: what recipients keep and use.
4. Custom Ink 2026 survey of 300+ employees: preferred products inside employee welcome kits.

Excluded: licensed characters, entertainment franchises, sports-team marks, celebrity likenesses, brand collaborations and replicas. All recommended directions use the buyer’s own company/event artwork or neutral patterns.

## Recommended first production batch for the site

1. Strengthen drinkware, bags and notebook/pen sets first because FY already has suitable catalog depth and conversion pages.
2. Add a mobile work tech kit using a verified power bank, cable and pouch SKU.
3. Build one apparel capsule: soft T-shirt, polo and hoodie/fleece, with clear size and fabric facts.
4. Add umbrella and blanket/towel pages only after real MOQ, material, branding, sample and lead-time evidence is available.
5. Do not make food gifts a first China-export landing page until shelf life, ingredients, labeling and destination customs rules are verified.

## Non-IP guardrails

- Use only customer-owned logos, event artwork, original neutral graphics or licensed stock elements.
- Reject cartoon/film/game characters, sports-club crests, celebrity images and imitation brand shapes unless written licensing evidence is supplied.
- Avoid product titles that trade on retail brands or viral product names; describe form and function generically.
- Record material, capacity, power rating, safety, MOQ and lead time only from verified SKU evidence.
