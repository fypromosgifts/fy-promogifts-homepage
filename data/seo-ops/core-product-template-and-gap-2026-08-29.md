# Seven Core Product Pages: Template and Gap Plan

Date: 2026-08-29

## Page template

Every indexable core-product page must contain:

1. One clear commercial intent and one canonical URL.
2. Real catalog products and real product imagery.
3. Verified material, size or capacity facts where the product record contains them.
4. MOQ, sample and lead-time language that is either verified for that exact SKU or explicitly described as project-led.
5. Relevant logo methods without claiming that every method suits every product.
6. Packaging and use-case guidance.
7. Visible FAQs that answer buyer questions.
8. Links to a related gift set, packaging route or inquiry path.
9. The shared attribution script so inquiries retain source-page and requested-product context.

## Remaining sourcing gaps

- A standalone heated mug with verified electrical specification, plug or power requirements, MOQ and packaging.
- A standalone branded towel range with material, dimensions, weight, logo method, MOQ and production timing.
- Verified product-level MOQ and production timing for the general tumbler and notebook range.

Until these facts are available, pages must say that MOQ and timing are confirmed after product, artwork, quantity, packaging, destination and deadline review.

- The Gift Sets catalog now has its own protected project-brief form, so buyers can request one of the three verified routes without returning to the homepage.

- The remaining sourcing gaps above stay open as evidence requirements, not as permission to publish thin or speculative pages.
