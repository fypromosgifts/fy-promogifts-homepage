Low MOQ promotional product FAQ

Does every product start from 30 pcs?

No. MOQ from 30 pcs applies to selected products. Final MOQ depends on item, material, logo method and packaging.

Can I order a sample first?

Samples are available for many products. Sample cost and timing depend on customization requirements.

Can low MOQ orders include custom packaging?

Sometimes. Simple packaging may be easier, while fully custom boxes may need higher MOQ.
