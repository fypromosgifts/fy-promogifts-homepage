FAQ

Employee Onboarding Kit Questions

Short answers for HR, admin, agency and promotional gift buyers before requesting a quotation.

What can be included in an employee onboarding kit?

Employee onboarding kits can include branded drinkware, notebooks, pens, canvas tote bags, ID badge holders, welcome cards, gift boxes, mailer boxes and other promotional products. The final selection should match your budget, onboarding process and delivery requirements.

Can I choose different products for the kit?

Yes. You can choose only the products you need, or ask FY PromoGifts to recommend a practical combination. Products, colors, logo methods and packaging can be adjusted before production.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Low MOQ options are available. Actual MOQ depends on the selected products, logo method, packaging and order requirements, so the best approach is to share your estimated quantity and preferred kit contents.

How quickly can I receive a digital mockup?

You can receive a free digital mockup within 2 hours after sharing your logo and requirements. The mockup helps confirm logo position, product direction and packaging presentation before ordering.

How long does production take?

Typical production takes 5-10 days after artwork approval, depending on the products, customization and packaging requirements. Shipping time should be confirmed separately based on destination and delivery method.

Can you customize the gift box and welcome card?

Yes. Gift boxes, mailer boxes, welcome cards, stickers, paper belly bands and inserts can be customized. Packaging can be adjusted to fit the selected products, presentation requirements and shipping method.

Can kits be shipped to multiple employee addresses?

Multiple-address delivery can be discussed based on destination, quantity, packing method and delivery date. Share the destination countries or regions early so the shipping plan can be checked before quotation.
