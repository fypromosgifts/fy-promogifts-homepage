FAQ

What should an employee welcome kit include?

Useful daily items such as drinkware, stationery, bags, and a welcome card are usually the safest starting point.

Can kits be shipped individually?

Project handling depends on destination and quantity. We can prepare gift-ready packaging for downstream shipping workflows.

Can we create different budget tiers?

Yes. The same kit concept can be adjusted with different product materials and packaging levels.
