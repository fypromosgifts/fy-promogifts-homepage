Buyer questions, answered by FY PromoGifts

What supplier red flags should I check before a first branded order?

Ask whether the supplier can state the exact product, branding and packaging configuration in writing; show a sample path; define what bulk output will be checked against; and explain how deviations are handled. Vague answers about materials, decoration, files or inspection are more important than a polished listing. Community question source.

Does a slow sample automatically mean the bulk order will be late?

Not automatically. A custom sample can include artwork checks, setup, material availability and decoration trials that do not repeat in the same way during bulk production. Ask for separate milestones for artwork approval, sample production, bulk production and inspection, then judge whether the explanations and dates stay consistent. Community question source.

Can FY PromoGifts suggest kit combinations?

Yes. Share the use case, audience, quantity and target budget. We can shortlist configurations from the Gift Sets catalog and align them with the branding and delivery plan.

Can I mix different product categories?

Yes. Drinkware, bags, stationery, accessories and packaging can be combined into one kit, provided the component quantities, fit and decoration methods work together.

Can kits be customized for different departments?

Yes. Product mix, card copy and packaging can be adjusted for different recipient groups. See the dedicated employee onboarding kit guide or client appreciation guide.

Quality-first kit decisions

Use fewer, better-matched items

A practical three- or four-item kit usually communicates more care than a crowded box of unrelated giveaways. Start with how the recipient works, travels or uses the gift.

Check logo durability by material

Printing, engraving and fired decal methods behave differently. Confirm the product surface, artwork size and expected use before choosing a branding method.

Approve the real configuration

A digital mockup checks direction; a physical sample checks color, scale, fit and finish. Agree which reference controls bulk production before approval.

Design packaging for delivery

A presentation box for hand delivery and a ship-ready kit have different protection needs. Box structure, inserts and outer cartons should follow the delivery route.
