Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy explains how FY PromoGifts handles personal information submitted through fypromogifts.com. It applies to website visitors and people who contact us about promotional products, branded gift kits, samples or quotations.

Information we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide in an inquiry, including your name, work email, WhatsApp or phone number, company, product interests, estimated quantity, need-by date, delivery destination, project message, and uploaded logo or artwork.

We may also collect basic technical and usage information such as page views, referring pages, approximate location, browser or device information, and interactions with catalog, contact and Kit Studio features.

How we use information

  • To answer inquiries and prepare product, customization, sample, packaging and shipping recommendations.
  • To prepare mockups, quotations and production-related communication requested by you.
  • To operate, secure, measure and improve the website and inquiry experience.
  • To maintain appropriate business records and comply with applicable obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use inquiry details to send unrelated marketing messages without an appropriate reason or permission.

Services that process information

The website uses service providers to operate its pages and forms. These may include Cloudflare for website delivery and security, Formspree for inquiry form processing, and Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager for site measurement. If you choose a WhatsApp link, your interaction is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms. These providers may process information under their own policies and service terms.

Cookies and measurement

The website may use cookies or similar technologies required for site operation, traffic measurement and attribution. You can limit optional cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may reduce measurement accuracy but should not prevent you from contacting us by email.

Retention and protection

We keep inquiry and project information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a requested project, maintain necessary business records, resolve disputes or meet applicable obligations. We use reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information you previously submitted, subject to any information we must retain for legitimate business or legal reasons. You may also choose not to provide optional fields and can contact us by email instead of using a web form.

International processing

Because FY PromoGifts serves international buyers and uses service providers in different locations, information may be processed outside your country. Privacy protections and legal requirements can vary by location.

Contact

For a privacy request or question, email info@fypromogifts.com. Please describe the request and the email address used for your inquiry so we can identify the relevant information.

Policy updates: We may revise this policy when website features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will remain available at this URL with its updated date.
