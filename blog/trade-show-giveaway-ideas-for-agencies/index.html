Start from the campaign moment

The best product is not always the most expensive one. It is the item that fits the audience, booth message and photo moment.

Keep the kit easy to execute

For event teams, reliable production and packing matter. Tote bags, lanyards, badges, stickers, pens and drinkware are easier to scale than complex custom objects.

Use color as the connector

A campaign can feel cohesive when the products share one main color and one accent color, even when the items are different.

Brief suppliers clearly

Share the event date, mood board, brand colors, quantity, target price and delivery point so the product options stay realistic.
