Best Employee Onboarding Kit Ideas for New Hires

A strong onboarding kit helps new hires feel prepared before the first meeting, especially when teams are remote or hybrid.

Published by FY PromoGifts | New hire gift planning

A strong onboarding kit helps new hires feel prepared before the first meeting, especially when teams are remote or hybrid.

Useful beats decorative

New hires are more likely to keep a tumbler, notebook, tote bag, pen or pouch than a purely decorative item. Build the kit around daily-use products.

Remote teams need better packaging

When a kit is shipped to home addresses, the unboxing moment matters more. A mailer box, insert card and tidy packing can make the company feel organized.

Create tiers when needed

A standard kit can work for most employees, while premium kits can be used for executives, sales teams or client-facing roles.
