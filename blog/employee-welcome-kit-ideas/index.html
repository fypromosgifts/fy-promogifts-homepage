A good onboarding kit should make the first day feel organized, personal and useful. The best kits combine everyday items, thoughtful packaging and a clear brand moment.

What to include in a new hire kit

Start with items people will actually use during the first month: drinkware, a notebook, a pen, a carry item and a welcome card. A smaller useful set usually feels better than a large box of random merchandise.

Three useful kit directions

Starter kits can use a notebook, pen and stickers. Standard kits can add a tumbler and tote bag. Premium kits can use a rigid box, drinkware, notebook, metal pen and a personalized card.

Make it feel custom without overbranding

Use one strong logo placement on the hero item, then repeat brand colors across the card, tissue, box or ribbon. This keeps the kit polished and wearable.

Before requesting a quote

Prepare quantity, delivery deadline, shipping country, logo file and the rough budget range. Those details help narrow the product mix quickly.
