Choose with intent

Three decisions that narrow the right style.

A good cap choice starts with the wearer and environment, not only the product photo.

01

Structured or relaxed

Choose a structured crown for uniforms and bold front embroidery, or a softer profile for lifestyle merchandise.

02

Cotton or technical fabric

Cotton supports a familiar everyday feel; quick-dry fabrics are better for active or warm-weather programs.

03

Curved or flat brim

Curved brims suit broad team use, while flat-brim profiles create a stronger streetwear direction.

Buyer questions

Before you request a quote.

What is the MOQ for custom baseball caps?

Standard logo customization starts from 50 caps. New constructions or complex trims are quoted separately.

Which logo methods work on baseball caps?

Embroidery, screen printing, heat transfer and selected digital print routes can be reviewed against the fabric, seams and artwork size.

Can I approve one sample first?

Yes. A paid one-piece custom sample is typically ready in 3-5 days, and the sample fee is refundable at 100+ caps.

How can the order be shipped?

Express, air freight, sea freight and DDP door-to-door can be arranged. Production times exclude international shipping.
