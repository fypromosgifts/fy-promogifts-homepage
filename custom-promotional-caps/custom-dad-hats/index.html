Choose with intent

Three decisions that narrow the right style.

A good cap choice starts with the wearer and environment, not only the product photo.

01

Clean washed or distressed

A clean wash feels versatile and premium; distressed details create a more casual vintage direction.

02

Cotton, denim or corduroy

Cotton is adaptable, denim adds visible character, and corduroy provides seasonal texture.

03

Quiet or high-contrast branding

Use tonal embroidery for understated merchandise or contrast thread and colorblocking for stronger campaign visibility.

Buyer questions

Before you request a quote.

What makes a cap a dad hat?

Dad hats typically use a softer, lower-profile crown, curved brim and adjustable closure, often with a washed or relaxed finish.

Can dad hats use embroidered logos?

Yes. Flat embroidery is common, while dimensional embroidery depends on crown softness, artwork and the selected fabric.

Can colors be matched to our campaign?

We can recommend available colors and coordinate the cap, thread and decoration direction. Exact custom dyeing or new materials are reviewed separately.

How can the order be shipped?

Express, air freight, sea freight and DDP door-to-door can be arranged. Production times exclude international shipping.
