Choose with intent

Three decisions that narrow the right style.

A good cap choice starts with the wearer and environment, not only the product photo.

01

Everyday or outdoor coverage

Classic cotton suits festivals and merchandise; wider brims and chin cords suit travel and field programs.

02

Cotton or quick-dry fabric

Cotton offers a familiar feel, while quick-dry fabric is more practical for heat, activity and repeated outdoor use.

03

Packable or structured finish

Packable styles travel easily; textured denim, waffle and fleece create a stronger seasonal merchandise story.

Buyer questions

Before you request a quote.

What is the MOQ for custom bucket hats?

Standard logo customization starts from 50 hats on proven structures. Reversible or fully customized programs may require separate review.

Where can a logo be placed on a bucket hat?

Front, side and selected brim positions can be reviewed. Placement depends on seams, brim construction and artwork size.

Are quick-dry and sun hats available?

Yes. The range includes quick-dry, perforated and wider-coverage outdoor options alongside cotton and lifestyle bucket hats.

How can the order be shipped?

Express, air freight, sea freight and DDP door-to-door can be arranged. Production times exclude international shipping.
