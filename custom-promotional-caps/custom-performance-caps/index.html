Choose with intent

Three decisions that narrow the right style.

A good cap choice starts with the wearer and environment, not only the product photo.

01

Laser perforation or mesh

Laser-cut panels create a clean technical look; mesh increases open airflow for field and summer programs.

02

Packable or shape-retaining

Foldable caps suit race kits and travel, while more structured profiles maintain a consistent branded silhouette.

03

Heat transfer or lightweight print

Low-bulk decoration is often better for technical fabrics; final method depends on coating, stretch and artwork detail.

Buyer questions

Before you request a quote.

What is the MOQ for custom performance caps?

Standard logo customization starts from 50 caps. Technical materials and fully customized specifications are confirmed per project.

Which branding method is best for quick-dry caps?

Heat transfer, screen printing and selected embroidery routes can be reviewed. The best method depends on fabric coating, panel construction and artwork.

Do production times include shipping?

No. Sampling is typically 3-5 days and bulk production 8-10 days after approval, excluding international shipping.

How can the order be shipped?

Express, air freight, sea freight and DDP door-to-door can be arranged. Production times exclude international shipping.
