Choose with intent

Three decisions that narrow the right style.

A good cap choice starts with the wearer and environment, not only the product photo.

01

Cotton or quick-dry fabric

Cotton suits casual golf and event programs; quick-dry polyester is better for races and active use.

02

Classic band or seamless shell

Traditional bands are familiar and adjustable, while seamless profiles create a cleaner technical look.

03

Standard or extended brim

Choose standard coverage for broad use or a larger curved brim for stronger sun-shade performance.

Buyer questions

Before you request a quote.

What is the MOQ for custom visors?

Standard logo customization starts from 50 visors. Fully customized structures are reviewed separately.

Can visors be embroidered?

Selected cotton and structured bands can support embroidery. Heat transfer or printing may be more suitable for seamless and quick-dry designs.

Can I order a single custom sample?

Yes. A paid one-piece sample is available and is typically ready in 3-5 days before international shipping.

How can the order be shipped?

Express, air freight, sea freight and DDP door-to-door can be arranged. Production times exclude international shipping.
