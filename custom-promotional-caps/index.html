Before you start

Three quick answers.

Is the cap design support really free?

Yes. We can prepare a visual mockup and practical decoration recommendation without a design fee. Physical custom samples are charged separately.

What does the 50-piece MOQ cover?

It applies to standard logo customization on a proven cap structure. Fully customized constructions, special trims or complex specifications are reviewed separately.

Do production times include shipping?

No. The typical 3-5 day sample time and 8-10 day bulk production time exclude international shipping. We can arrange express, air, sea or DDP delivery.
