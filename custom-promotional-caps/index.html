Buyer questions

Know the terms before you start.

Is the design service really free?

Yes. Send us your logo, brand colors or a simple idea and we will prepare a free visual cap mockup. Physical custom samples are charged separately.

What is the MOQ for custom caps?

Standard logo customization starts from 50 caps. Fully customized constructions are reviewed separately.

Can I order just one sample?

Yes. A paid 1-piece custom sample is available. The sample fee is refundable when the bulk order reaches 100 caps.

What are the lead times?

Sampling is typically 3-5 days. Bulk production is typically 8-10 days after artwork or sample approval. International shipping time is not included.

Which shipping methods are available?

Express, air freight, sea freight and DDP door-to-door can be arranged according to destination, deadline and shipment size.
