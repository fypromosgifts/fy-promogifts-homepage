Choose with intent

Three decisions that narrow the right style.

A good cap choice starts with the wearer and environment, not only the product photo.

01

Foam, cotton or premium front

Foam creates a classic promotional look, cotton is versatile, and soft-touch fronts suit premium merchandise.

02

Curved or flat brim

Curved profiles are easy for team wear; flat brims create a stronger youth and retail direction.

03

Standard or performance mesh

Choose open mesh for everyday airflow or technical perforation for active programs.

Buyer questions

Before you request a quote.

What is the MOQ for custom trucker hats?

Standard logo customization starts from 50 hats. Final MOQ can vary for special materials or fully customized constructions.

Can trucker hats be embroidered?

Many cotton, foam and soft-touch front panels can support embroidery. Heat transfer or printing may be recommended for lightweight performance fronts.

How quickly can I receive a sample?

A paid one-piece custom sample is typically ready in 3-5 days before international shipping.

How can the order be shipped?

Express, air freight, sea freight and DDP door-to-door can be arranged. Production times exclude international shipping.
