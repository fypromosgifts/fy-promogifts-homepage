FAQ

Custom Notebook Questions

Practical answers for HR, procurement, agency and event buyers before requesting a quotation.

What notebook styles can be customized?

Common options include PU leather notebooks, hardcover journals, softcover notebooks, planners, spiral notebooks, cork notebooks and notebook gift sets. Availability depends on quantity, specifications and delivery requirements.

What logo methods work on custom notebooks?

Logo options may include debossing, embossing, foil stamping, screen printing, UV printing, printed sleeves and custom packaging. The best method depends on the cover material, artwork and quantity.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Low MOQ options are available. Actual MOQ depends on the notebook style, cover color, page customization, logo method and packaging, so we confirm it after the specification is selected.

Can the inside pages be customized?

Yes. Depending on construction and quantity, options may include a branded title page, custom inserts, ruled or dotted layouts, planners, repeated logos and custom endpapers.

Can notebooks be combined with pens or drinkware?

Yes. Notebooks pair well with pens, tumblers, bags, welcome cards and gift boxes for onboarding, event and client gift kits.

How quickly can I receive a digital mockup?

A free digital mockup can usually be prepared within 2 hours after receiving the logo, preferred notebook style and estimated quantity.

How long does production take?

Typical production takes about 7-12 days after artwork approval, depending on notebook construction, page printing, logo method, packaging and quantity. Shipping time is confirmed separately.

Can you provide individual or gift-box packaging?

Yes. Options may include individual bags, paper sleeves, kraft boxes, notebook-and-pen boxes, custom gift boxes and mixed-product kit assembly.
