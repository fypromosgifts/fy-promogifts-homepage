Buyer Questions

Custom Promotional Gifts FAQ

Quick answers for agencies, event planners and corporate buyers comparing promotional gift suppliers, MOQ, logo methods and delivery timing.

What promotional gifts can FY PromoGifts customize?

FY PromoGifts customizes drinkware, notebooks, pens, tote bags, caps, T-shirts, lanyards, keychains, stickers, gift boxes and mixed-product branded gift kits. Buyers can request one product or a complete kit for events, onboarding and client gifting.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Low MOQ is available for selected products. MOQ varies by product, logo method, packaging, and order requirements.

How fast can I get a mockup and quote?

Free digital mockup within 2 hours is available after receiving your logo, quantity, deadline and product idea. Production timing depends on the item, order requirements and customization method.

Can you build a complete branded gift kit?

Yes. Share your audience, event date, budget and brand style. FY PromoGifts can recommend product combinations, logo printing methods, insert cards, packaging options and shipping-friendly kit layouts.

Which logo files should I send?

Vector artwork such as AI, PDF, EPS or SVG is best for production. JPG and PNG can also be used for early mockups. If the file is not ready, simple artwork adjustment can be discussed before sampling.

Do you support samples and safer payment terms?

Sample orders are supported for many products before bulk production. Alibaba Trade Assurance can be discussed on request, and payment terms are confirmed based on the product, quantity and order schedule.
